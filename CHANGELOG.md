## 1.0.0

First public release

- pull 3 to 12 colours out of an uploaded, dropped or pasted image, or out of the images (as you see them, crop and rotation included) or shapes you have selected on the board
- see each colour's hex, share, nearest CSS name and contrast against white and #111827 with AA and AAA levels
- Vibrant, Muted, Dark vibrant and Light muted roles
- sort by share, lightness or hue, and click any colour to copy its hex
- export as HEX, CSS custom properties, Tailwind 3, Tailwind 4 or JSON
- place a swatch row on the board as equal squares or a share bar, under what it was read from or in the middle of your view, at the first free spot, and select it
- swatches close to the board colour get a thin grey edge
- edit a placed palette later and update it in place, keeping its group, frame, layer, opacity and lock, or place it as new
- ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z work from the panel, and the panel follows the board theme live
- asks for storage and board access on first open, not at page load
- settings are remembered between sessions
